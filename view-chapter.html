1 John 5 Draft
Resources
5:1
"Born of God" describes spiritual regeneration. Everyone who trusts that Jesus is the Messiah has been spiritually reborn through God. This sonship creates a family bond with other believers.
5:4
"The world" here refers to the system of values and powers that oppose God. Faith is the means by which believers gain the victory that Christ has already won. Alternate translation: "conquers the world."
5:6
Most interpreters understand "water" as Jesus' baptism and "blood" as his crucifixion, emphasizing that Jesus came as a fully human savior who both began his ministry and died a real death.
5:16
This is a difficult passage. The "sin leading to death" has been interpreted as deliberate apostasy or willful rejection of Christ. John does not define it precisely. The passage encourages intercession for fellow believers.
