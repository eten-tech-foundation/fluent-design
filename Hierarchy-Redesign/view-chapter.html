1:1
"Genealogy" refers to a list of ancestors. Matthew begins with this to establish Jesus as a descendant of both David and Abraham, fulfilling the covenant promises made to them.
1:16
Note the shift in wording: every other person in the list "fathered" the next, but here it says Joseph was "the husband of Mary, of whom Jesus was born." This passive construction avoids saying Joseph fathered Jesus.
1:21
The name "Jesus" (Yeshua) means "the Lord saves." Matthew makes explicit the purpose of Jesus' coming: salvation from sin, not merely political deliverance.
1:23
"Immanuel" is a Hebrew name meaning "God with us." Matthew quotes Isaiah 7:14 to show the birth of Jesus fulfills this prophecy. If your language has a word for "God," consider how best to render this meaningful name.
